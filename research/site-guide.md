## Coverage and limits

The linked prize microsite has three distinct substantive HTML pages: home, rules and FAQ. Home also has a byte-identical `index.htm` alias. The [page manifest](site/pages.tsv) identifies source body hashes and failures; the [link inventory](site/links.tsv) preserves original and resolved targets (including anchors, media and external destinations). There are 547 href/src occurrences across the three distinct pages, 818 if the duplicate index is counted. [Resource checks](site/resources.tsv) cover 33 distinct prize-local URLs including the two historical pledge forms. Five returned 404: three bare FAQ links (`compai`, `compuse`, `perplexity`), a malformed external bibliography link treated as a local path, and the prospective `award12.gif`. The actual FAQ entries can be located by searching the FAQ text. A missing prospective certificate is not evidence of a twelfth award.

These are counts of the visible link graph, **not** a server file listing or a claim to have read every linked book, viewed every frame of every video, decoded every historical submission or reviewed every external forum reply. The text extracts preserve content for search, not visual layout; open the HTML for superscripts, equations, links and table alignment. Run `python research/tools/site_inventory.py` to refresh the visible link graph, which overwrites the text extracts and page/link inventories; `resources.tsv` is a separately dated check. Follow the research chapters for synthesis rather than reading the manifests linearly.
